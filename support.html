What to include in a support request

  • Browser: Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • Hub Assistant version, if visible.
  • The general PowerSchool area where the issue occurred, such as Messages or Newsfeed.
  • A short description of what you expected and what happened.
  • A sanitized screenshot only if needed.

Protect school data when asking for help

Do not send passwords, session tokens, authentication cookies, or other credentials.

Do not include real student or staff personal information unless your school's authorized support process specifically requires it. Prefer sanitized screenshots and synthetic examples.
